# Synthetic Ukraine-Russia Report Expansion

## Status

Implemented on 2 August 2026.

## Goal

Expand the local, downloadable synthetic intelligence library with enough
Ukraine-Russia themed material to demonstrate realistic Store and RFI searches
across 2025 and 2026 without representing any real intelligence reporting.

This specification extends the
[synthetic intelligence library](synthetic-intelligence-library-and-search-assurance.md).

## Baseline audit

Before this expansion, the running local application contained 217 products,
193 PDF assets and 45 Russia-themed PDF products. All 144 PDFs in the original
deterministic `PROD-3xxx` corpus had integrity metadata, object bytes and a
successful passage-index state. The weakness was subject coverage: those
reports described generic fictional exercises and contained no Ukraine,
Kursk, Donbas, Donetsk, Kyiv, Moscow, Kharkiv or Zaporizhzhia scenarios.

## Scope

- Add 72 deterministic, four-page PDF products across 12 synthetic scenarios.
- Cover Kursk, northern Donbas, Donetsk, Luhansk, Kharkiv, Zaporizhzhia, Kyiv,
  the Black Sea and Moscow.
- Cover armour, artillery, logistics, electronic warfare, missiles, drones,
  air defence, counter-UAS and warning indicators.
- Spread report periods across 2025 and 2026.
- Reuse the existing Russia capability ACGs so the established need-to-know
  policy continues to govern browse, preview, download and RFI results.
- Improve generated PDF narratives so judgements, indicators and collection
  gaps reflect each product's region, period and themes.
- Seed existing local databases idempotently and materialise real PDF bytes in
  local object storage.

## Scenario matrix

The collection contains six report variants for each scenario:

1. Kursk border-area ground activity.
2. Northern Donbas mechanised movement.
3. Donetsk artillery and sustainment activity.
4. Luhansk logistics-route activity.
5. Kharkiv border-area warning indicators.
6. Zaporizhzhia southern-corridor activity.
7. Kyiv missile warning patterns.
8. Kyiv drone and counter-UAS activity.
9. Black Sea missile and maritime-drone activity.
10. Moscow drone warning and counter-UAS activity.
11. Moscow regional air-defence activity.
12. Donbas electronic-warfare activity.

The six variants are a situation update, activity-pattern assessment,
indicators and warning note, strike or effects digest, logistics and readiness
note, and thirty-day outlook. Their coverage windows span January 2025 to July 2026.

## Synthetic-data safety

- Every page carries one `SYNTHETIC EXERCISE PRODUCT` marker. The authenticated
  shell and product release metadata provide the corresponding Store context,
  so titles do not carry a redundant prefix.
- Place names provide search realism, but observations, timelines, source
  descriptions and judgements are invented.
- Reports contain no real unit identities, coordinates, target details,
  technical parameters, source reporting or operational recommendations.
- Metadata retains `MOCK` releasability and the `MOCK DATA ONLY` caveat.
- The generator is deterministic and uses no network source or generative
  model.

## Search and lifecycle behaviour

Publishing the expanded seed changes the corpus fingerprint and truthfully
marks the existing passage index stale. The previous generation remains
available as labelled partial coverage. A successful rebuild atomically
promotes a new generation containing all extractable report passages.

Product metadata and product-level embeddings are persisted during seed
upsert, so ordinary Store and RFI candidate search can find the new products
before passage rebuilding finishes. Deep PDF evidence becomes current after
the rebuild.

## Acceptance criteria

- A fresh local catalogue contains exactly 261 products, including 216
  deterministic `PROD-3xxx` PDF products.
- Exactly 72 products belong to the Ukraine-Russia expansion and cover all 12
  scenarios and both years.
- Every expansion product is published, assigned to one active ACG, marked
  `MOCK DATA ONLY`, and has one valid four-page PDF with matching size and
  SHA-256 metadata.
- Representative Store searches for Kursk activity, Donbas armour, Donetsk
  artillery, Kyiv missiles, Kyiv drones, Moscow drones and Black Sea activity
  return authorised expansion products.
- A restricted Russia SIGINT product remains hidden from a customer without
  that ACG.
- An authenticated authorised user can obtain a grant and download a seeded
  PDF with `application/pdf`, `Content-Disposition: attachment` and
  `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- Representative PDFs render cleanly with no clipping, overlap or unreadable
  glyphs.
- Re-seeding does not duplicate products and repairs missing or corrupt
  generated bytes.
- Rebuilding retrieval reaches `ready` with matching request-document and
  request-vector counts and no missing expansion assets.
- Repository line, branch, documentation, security and file-size gates pass.
